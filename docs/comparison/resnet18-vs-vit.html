Does the result depend on the network?

Not the main result. A network that seemed to have forgotten CIFAR-10 relearned it about 7 points better than an equally trained control, with a ResNet-18 and with a Vision Transformer, in every seed. What differs is what else each network keeps: the ViT keeps more general photo knowledge, looks less forgotten by its old head, and shows a small memory of specific photos.

Why they might differ

Old head: not BatchNorm

Idea: SVHN replaced ResNet-18's BatchNorm averages, which the ViT's LayerNorm doesn't keep. Tested and ruled out: recomputing ResNet-18's BatchNorm statistics on CIFAR-10 leaves A's old head at 16.3% (18.8% as saved). Better candidate: overwrite strength. The ViT's old head reaches 20% at its strongest stage-2 rate, ResNet-18's level.

General knowledge

The ViT keeps more general features through SVHN, but they help less when relearning from 5k photos. Where in the network this happens is open: probing each block (Q3) would show it.

Memory of photos

The ViT shows a small, consistent advantage on the exact photos it trained on (~1.8 points); ResNet-18 does not. Small effect, 3 seeds: a direct per-image test after stage 2 would confirm it.

Caveats

Different recipes

Optimizer, augmentation, warmups and lengths differ, so raw accuracies are not comparable across networks.

Different overwrite

ResNet-18: 4 SVHN epochs, set by A's old head. ViT: 20, set by B reaching 95% on SVHN.

Adapted rules

The ViT's old-head rule and stage-2 length rule were adapted, before any relearning result (see the ViT report).

Small scale

3 seeds each, 32×32 images, one size per network, 5k relearning photos.
